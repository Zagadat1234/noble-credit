Privacy Policy - Noble Credit

Welcome to Noble Credit Finance Company Limited (Noble Credit). This Notice defines requirements to help ensure compliance with laws and regulations applicable to Noble Credit collection, storage, use, transmission, disclosure to third parties, and retention of personal information and sensitive personal data (also referred to as sensitive personal information).

Personal information means any information relating to or which identifies an individual (“you or your”). This can include items such as your name, address, phone number, identification numbers (such as an account number, Bank Verification Number (BVN), Tax Identification Number (TIN), Staff ID, Integrated Payroll and Personnel Information System Number (IPPIS Number), National Identification Number (NIN)), location data or online identifiers including information collected through the use of our website and cookies.

We require your personal information to comply with our business, legal and regulatory obligations or enter into an agreement with you. We may not be able to provide you with the products or services you have requested if you do not provide us with your personal information.

This Privacy Notice (“Notice”) is issued in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation 2019 (NDPR), the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and seeks to explain:

Who we are

Noble Credit is a CBN duly licensed finance company based in Nigeria that provides swift, easily accessible loan and investment products to customers. We endeavour to meet leading standards for data protection and privacy. While our reasons are founded in ethical and corporate responsibility, our privacy practices as outlined in this Notice facilitate the following:
  • Competitive Advantage: Our emphasis on protecting the privacy of prospective customers, customers, vendors, and employees distinguishes us from our competitors.
  • Good Corporate Citizenship: A sound privacy notice is emblematic of reliable corporate citizens that respect individuals’ privacy.
  • Business Enablement: Since Credit Direct uses significant volumes of personal information, privacy notices become a prerequisite to building enduring business relationships.
  • Legal Protection: Appropriate privacy notices offer an opportunity to eliminate allegations of unlawful usage of personal information.
